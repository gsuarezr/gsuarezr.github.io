I’m a Venezuelan physicist, with passion for problem solving and software development. 
I’m currently an [IPD-STEMA fellow](https://www.recherche.uliege.be/cms/c_13352123/en/ipd-stema)
in the [University of Liege](https://www.uliege.be/cms/c_8699436/en/uliege). My
work focuses on the description of Open Quantum Systems, particularly in 
Non-Markovian equations focusing on the [Refined Weak coupling/ExpZ map/ cumulant equation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.05776) and 
other techniques for the description of open systems such as [HEOM](https://pubs.aip.org/aip/jcp/article/153/2/020901/76291/Numerically-exact-approach-to-open-quantum), [The Reaction Coordinate mapping](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.08307), 
[The time convolutionless equation (TCL)](https://indico.ictp.it/event/a10127/session/15/contribution/10/material/0/0.pdf), [Pseudomodes]() … etc. 
A quick introduction to those techniques can be found in my [PhD thesis](https://old.mfi.ug.edu.pl/sites/default/files/postepowania_naukowe/119034/praca/thesis.pdf)

I started off my career as an Electronics Engineer,

later I did my PhD in open quantum systems at [ICTQT](https://ictqt.ug.edu.pl/) under the 
supervision of [Michał Horodecki](https://ictqt.ug.edu.pl/pages/new-quantum-resources-and-thermodynamics/). 
I had the fortune to be an intern at [Franco Nori's group in RIKEN](https://dml.riken.jp/)
where I worked on [the QuTiP environment class](https://qutip.readthedocs.io/en/latest/guide/guide-environments.html), 
under the supervision of [Neill Lambert](https://nwlambert.wordpress.com/) and [Paul Menczel](https://www.menczel.net/).
